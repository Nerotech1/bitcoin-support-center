Why V-Card?

We believe in empowering our users to do more with crypto in life, work, and play. We do this by making crypto easy, fun, and, rewarding to use. And that’s why we’re launching our first Web3 prepaid card for our users to go from crypto to cash in a flash.

What is V-Card?

V-Card is a web3 prepaid card which you can top up with crypto and spend your balance anywhere Mastercard is accepted.

How can I get a V-Card?

Overview

  1. Download the Bitcoin.com Wallet app if you haven’t already

    Get App

  2. Top up your wallet with one or more of the cryptocurrencies supported by V-Card. These are: BTC, BCH, ETH, USDC, USDT, and VERSE.

  3. Purchase your V-Card using one of the above cryptocurrencies. You’ll login in with your email, complete ID verification, and pay for the card. And remember, if you buy using Bitcoin.com’s ecosystem token VERSE, you’ll get a 33% discount!

Step-by-step instructions for ordering your V-Card

1. Login with your email at card.verse.bitcoin.com

2. Enter your email address. You will receive a one-time verification code in your email. Enter the code to log in.

3. Tap on “Order a Physical Card” to begin the application process.

4. Complete the Know-your Customer (KYC) process by providing the requested identity document and performing a liveness check. Approvals will take 2-5 business days. You are able to check the status of your KYC approval in the user dashboard. If you encounter any issues with completing the KYC process, please contact support@bitcoin.com.

5. Once approved, tap on “Order a Physical Card” to begin the order process.

  1. Check that your personal details are correct. Some fields are filled automatically based on the KYC document that you provided. If there are any discrepancies, please contact support@bitcoin.com.

  2. Enter your Consignee and Delivery Address where the card will be delivered to

6. Review the card application details, recipient information, and payment for the card application fee. This card application fee covers opening of your account and production of your web3 debit card. We will ship V-Cards globally for free.

  1. You are able to pay for the card application fee with popular tokens such as BTC, BCH, ETH, USDT and more. The card costs 74.99 EUR when paying with these tokens.

  2. Enjoy a discounted card application fee at 49.99 EUR when paying with Bitcoin.com's ecosystem token VERSE.

7. Tap on the Pay button. You will be taken to a payment page. Review the payment details. Send the requested amount to the address on the screen using your Bitcoin.com Wallet app.

You may also scan the QR code using the Bitcoin.com Wallet app. Ensure that you are sending the right token eg. VERSE on Polygon.

Once the payment for the application fee has been completed, we will ship your card to your delivery address in 10-14 business days.

Tips for passing KYC

Since the KYC is an automated authentication process, please ensure you:

  • have adequate lighting when taking photos

  • upload documents that match the selected information you typed in

  • users need to be over 18 and under 65 years old to apply for the card.

What are the supported documents for KYC?

  • In European EEA countries, both identity cards and passports are supported.
    For non-European EEA users, only passports are supported.

EEA countries are as follows:
Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.

How long does the KYC review take?

KYC takes approximately 1-30 minutes. If it takes longer to get approved, please reach out to our customer service at support@bitcoin.com or in the support chat within the Bitcoin.com Wallet app and provide the following information:

  • Your registered email address

  • Screenshots related to KYC error

What should I do if I am unable to pass the KYC process?

If you fail after multiple attempts, please reach out to our customer service at support@bitcoin.com or in the support chat within the Bitcoin.com Wallet app and provide the following information:

  • Your registered email address

I’ve received my card. How do I activate my card?

  1. Visit card.verse.bitcoin.com

  2. Log in with your registered email and verification code

  3. Tap “Activate my Physical Card” and follow the on-screen instructions

How do I add money to my card?

  1. Visit card.verse.bitcoin.com

  2. Log in with your registered email and verification code.

  3. Tap “Top up card.”

  4. Enter the amount to add to your balance.

  5. Select the payment currency and network (eg. fxVERSE on Polygon)

  6. Copy the recharge cryptocurrency address displayed.

  7. Open your Bitcoin.com Wallet app and send the correct amount of cryptocurrency to the recharge address. Please see step-by-step instructions for sending cryptocurrency.

When will the monthly management fee be deducted?

The monthly fee is deducted on the 30th of every month at 22:00 UTC+2. Note that if you do not have a balance on your card at the time the management fee is deducted, the outstanding fee will be deducted at the time of the next recharge.

How do I deactivate my lost or stolen card?

1. Visit card.verse.bitcoin.com and log in.
2. Select "Report Loss".
3. Select the "Cancel Card" option.
4. Enter your card number.
5. Provide the reason for canceling the card.
6. Upload a photo of yourself holding your ID card or passport as proof of identity.
7. Submit the request to deactivate your card.

Can the PIN Be Reset?

The PIN cannot be modified or reset. However, if you've entered the wrong PIN multiple times, follow the steps below to view the correct PIN:

  1. Log in to the card homepage at card.verse.bitcoin.com.

  2. Click [View PIN].

  3. Verify the account 2FA.

How to change the OTP phone number for your V-Card

To update the phone number used for receiving OTPs on your V-Card, please contact the Bitcoin.com Support Team.

In your message, include the following details:

  • Your registered email address

  • Your old mobile number

  • Your new mobile number

Our support team will escalate your request to the third-party service provider, to complete the phone number change.
